about me

Cade is a designer, builder, and web tinkerer working on ideas that are a little weird, a little useful, and hopefully memorable. This site is a place for experiments, personal projects, and the kind of creative work that exists outside of a rigid template.

The goal is simple: make the internet feel a little more personal, a little less polished, and a lot more like a real person built it.

The left side is intentionally left ready for the attached portrait image. The right side is where the story can live.
